# 1. Purpose

Execution Intelligence determines whether the operating system is being performed consistently enough to produce the expected financial, operational, and customer outcomes.

It converts process measurements into an assessment of:

- Operating-standard compliance
- Workflow health
- Execution breakdowns
- Recoverable operational opportunities
- Management intervention requirements
- Expected impact of improved execution

Execution Intelligence does not merely report whether a task was completed.

It determines whether the actions required to produce results are occurring at the right time, in the right sequence, and at the expected level.

---

# 2. Primary Management Question

Execution Intelligence answers:

> Is the operating system being executed, and where must leadership intervene?

Supporting questions include:

- Are required actions occurring?
- Are they occurring at the right time?
- Are they being completed consistently?
- Where is the operating process breaking down?
- Which execution failure is affecting results?
- Which leader, team, role, or workflow requires intervention?
- What measurable outcome should improve if execution is corrected?

---

# 3. Scope

Execution Intelligence owns measurements and assessments related to operational discipline and process performance.

It includes:

- Welcome Pack preparation and usage
- MPI completion
- MPI timing
- Video creation
- Video viewing
- Advisor presentation
- Manager turnover on identified safety items
- Task completion
- Task completion under 60 minutes
- Warranty-to-customer-pay conversion
- Tires
- Alignments
- Batteries
- Brakes
- Same-day conversion
- Declined-service follow-up
- No-show recovery
- Dispatch execution
- Completed-status management
- Open repair-order management
- Daily operating cadence
- Leadership inspection points
- Process exceptions

It does not own:

- Production capacity
- Financial valuation
- Customer satisfaction scoring
- Technician dependency risk
- Organizational development
- Cross-domain recommendation prioritization

Those responsibilities belong to other Management Intelligence™ domains.

---

# 4. Domain Boundary

Execution Intelligence evaluates whether the required operating actions are occurring.

Production Intelligence evaluates whether sufficient production will be achieved.

Financial Intelligence evaluates the economic value of execution.

Customer Intelligence evaluates the client impact of execution.

Organizational Intelligence evaluates whether execution capability is sustainable.

The Recommendation Engine determines which execution issue should receive management priority.

## Key Outcomes

- Intelligence must improve a real management decision rather than merely display data.
- Facts, interpretations, and recommendations must remain distinct.
- Material conclusions must be explainable and uncertainty must remain visible.
- Recommendations must account for capacity, constraints, controllability, impact, and urgency.
- Human judgment is augmented rather than replaced.
- Outcomes complete the intelligence loop through observation, action, verification, and learning.
- Architecture must preserve canonical language, objects, boundaries, and ownership as the platform grows.

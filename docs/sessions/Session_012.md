# Major Architectural Decisions (ADR)

## ADR-012-001

Decision:

Management Intelligence is implemented as a Decision Operating System rather than an analytics platform.

Rationale:

Organizations improve through disciplined decisions, not through reports alone.

Impact:

Every feature must ultimately support better management decisions.

---

## ADR-012-002

Decision:

Leadership attention is treated as a constrained organizational resource.

Rationale:

Executives cannot effectively manage unlimited recommendations.

Impact:

Recommendations compete for attention through a Recommendation Portfolio.

---

## ADR-012-003

Decision:

Decision Quality, Execution Quality, and Outcome Quality are evaluated independently.

Rationale:

Business outcomes alone cannot accurately evaluate leadership effectiveness.

Impact:

Validation becomes objective rather than outcome-biased.

---

## ADR-012-004

Decision:

Decision Effectiveness Score (DES) becomes the canonical measurement of management capability.

Rationale:

Leadership effectiveness should be measurable independently of operational KPIs.

Impact:

The platform measures management capability rather than only business performance.

---

## ADR-012-005

Decision:

Organizational Learning closes every Management Decision.

Rationale:

Completed decisions should permanently improve future recommendations and organizational capability.

Impact:

Management Intelligence becomes a continuously learning system.

# Key Insight

The platform is no longer centered on reporting.

It is no longer centered solely on artificial intelligence.

It is centered on disciplined Management Decisions.

Operational reality becomes intelligence.

Intelligence becomes recommendations.

Recommendations support Management Decisions.

Management Decisions create execution.

Execution produces measurable outcomes.

Validation captures organizational learning.

Organizational learning continuously improves future decisions.

This closed-loop architecture defines the Management Intelligence Decision Operating System.

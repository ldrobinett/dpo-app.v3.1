→ Condition → Causal Diagnosis → Recommendation / Intervention →
Confidence + Materiality + Concern + Urgency → Critical / Need / Nice →

3.1 Signal & Condition Detection --- FROZEN

Core rule: True Potential is the primary economic performance mile
marker for MI. Budget/objective is a structural stress test of True
Potential, not an alternative primary baseline.

Reasoning flow: Trusted Evidence → Potential Integrity Test → True
Potential → Objective↔TP Structural Stress Test → TP↔Forecast
Realization → TP↔Actual Realization → Variance/Trend/Pattern/Expected
Change → Signal → Evidence Quality + Materiality + Context → Condition.

Frozen Principles

True Potential Primacy: Forecast and Actual are evaluated
against TP to determine realization, opportunity and material
Conditions.

Objective Stress Test: Objective/Budget tests whether governed
capacity, demand and economics support organizational expectation.

True Potential Input Integrity: TP is authoritative only when
governed inputs are current, complete and compliant with canonical
methodology.

DPO Governance Integrity: Technician DPO is reviewed monthly
using canonical methodology. Sustained production inconsistent with
governed DPO triggers validity/governance review.

Potential Integrity Test: Validate material TP inputs before
using TP as the mile marker.

Intent Neutrality: Detect observable governance deviation
independently from inferred human intent.

Mathematical Integrity: Validated evidence and governed
mathematics are not altered to reconcile unsupported human
narrative.

Narrative Validation: Human explanations are evidence to test,
not authoritative conclusions.

Fact-Conjecture Separation: Distinguish fact, supported
inference, hypothesis and conjecture.

Additional rules: Context Before Condition; Direction-Neutral Reasoning;
Expected-Change Reasoning.

3.2 Causal Diagnosis --- FROZEN

Core question: Why is this happening?

MI moves upstream from the observed outcome toward the most materially
supported cause management can meaningfully influence.

A downstream metric such as low Hrs/RO is not automatically a diagnosis.

Management Attention Classification --- Critical / Need / Nice

Covey-style urgency/importance reasoning provides useful conceptual
grounding, while Critical / Need / Nice (CNN) is V5's default
management-facing operating language.

Canonical reasoning remains underneath:

Materiality + Urgency + Concern + Actionability + Recoverability +
Consequence + Management Horizon → Management Attention Classification →
Critical / Need / Nice.

3.4.6 Attention Classification

MI classifies management work using materiality, urgency, Concern,
actionability, recoverability, consequence and management horizon.
Canonical reasoning remains independent of tenant-facing terminology.

3.4.7 Critical

Action is required within the immediate management horizon because delay
creates material consequence, materially reduces recoverability, or
closes an important action window.

Critical includes both immediate consequence and a closing
recovery/action window even when the final consequence occurs later.

3.4.8 Need

Action is required within a defined near-term horizon because continued
delay is reasonably expected to increase Concern, reduce recoverability
or elevate the Condition to Critical.

3.4.9 Nice

Action has supported value but can be deferred without a currently
supported material consequence. Nice means useful, but not now.

Digital FOD should give management the smallest number of things
genuinely requiring attention, organized by consequence of delay. If
more than three independently Critical Conditions exist, Critical
Override applies.

Explanation Principles

Progressive Explanation: Present the minimum explanation
necessary for the recipient while preserving deeper reasoning and
evidence lineage.

Truth Consistency: Explanation depth may vary by role;
underlying facts and causal conclusions do not.

Explicit Uncertainty: Communicate uncertainty that could affect
a management decision.

Decision-Relevant Uncertainty: Prominently surface uncertainty
only when it could materially change diagnosis, recommendation or
decision.

Mathematical Traceability: Preserve formula, input and source
lineage for material quantitative conclusions.

Epistemic Discipline: Preserve distinctions among fact,
supported inference, hypothesis, unknown and conjecture.

A material recommendation should answer: 1. What matters? 2. Why? 3.
What evidence supports it? 4. What did MI rule out or weaken? 5. What
should management do? 6. What should happen, and by when, if MI is
right?

Session 030 Architectural Decisions

True Potential remains the primary economic mile marker.

TP validity depends on governed input integrity.

Mathematical contradiction outranks unsupported narrative.

MI separates fact, inference, hypothesis and conjecture.

Diagnosis proceeds upstream to actionable causal depth.

MI tests competing hypotheses and disconfirming evidence.

Multiple supported causes may coexist.

Recommendations must connect directly to diagnosis.

Recommendations include owner, timing, expected outcome and review
horizon.

Immediate recovery and durable correction are separate intervention
purposes.

Human alternative actions are legitimate learning evidence.

Recommendation quality remains separate from eventual outcome.

Confidence, Materiality, Concern and Urgency remain distinct.

Quantitative conclusions preserve mathematical traceability.

Critical / Need / Nice is the default V5 management-facing
attention model.

CNN is a presentation layer over canonical reasoning, not a
replacement for the canonical model.

Digital FOD surfaces the smallest number of actions genuinely
requiring management attention.

# Session Objective

Establish the canonical architecture for how Management Intelligence domains reason, generate recommendations, and improve management decision quality.

This session transitions the platform from defining business structure to defining business intelligence.

---

# Major Architectural Decisions

## 1. Introduced Domain Intelligence

Established the concept of independent Intelligence Domains.

Each domain is responsible for:

- Business knowledge
- Measurements
- Signals
- Insights
- Recommendations
- Management Decision support
- Validation
- Organizational Learning

Every domain follows the same reasoning architecture while maintaining domain-specific expertise.

---

## 2. Created the Service Intelligence Domain

Established Service Intelligence as the reference implementation for all future domains.

Service Intelligence defines:

- Business responsibilities
- Business objects
- Measurements
- Leading indicators
- Lagging indicators
- Signals
- Intelligence questions
- Management decisions
- Execution standards
- Validation
- Organizational learning

Future domains should mirror this structure.

---

## 3. Established the AI Reasoning Model

Created a canonical reasoning framework shared by every Intelligence Domain.

Reasoning Flow:

Business Objects

↓

Measurements

↓

Observations

↓

Signals

↓

Insights

↓

Risks & Opportunities

↓

Recommendation Output

↓

Management Decision

↓

Execution

↓

Validation

↓

Business Outcomes

↓

Organizational Learning

This reasoning model becomes the cognitive architecture of Management Intelligence.

---

## 4. Defined Canonical Reasoning Patterns

Established reusable reasoning models for every Intelligence Domain.

Patterns include:

- Constraint Analysis
- Trend Analysis
- Variance Analysis
- Root Cause Analysis
- Comparative Analysis
- Pattern Recognition
- Decision Recall
- Leverage Analysis
- Risk Forecasting
- Opportunity Forecasting
- Counterfactual Reasoning
- Validation Reasoning

These reasoning patterns provide consistency across all domains.

---

## 5. Separated Intelligence from Leadership

Reinforced a foundational architectural principle:

The platform recommends.

Leadership decides.

Management Decisions remain accountable human decisions supported by evidence rather than automated conclusions.

# Session Summary

Session 011 represents the transition from architecture to intelligence.

Management Intelligence is no longer simply a system that stores business information.

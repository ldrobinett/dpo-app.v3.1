Purpose

This session memorializes the post-domain consolidation review of
Management Intelligence™ V5. The twelve domains had already been
individually defined and smoke-tested. Domains 2--12 were then
regression-vetted against the consolidated canonical object model and
revalidated from a single-rooftop dealership through small-group,
multi-store, and enterprise structures.

MI scales from the rooftop outward, not from the enterprise
downward.

Validation Standard

Each domain was tested for canonical-object fit, one-rooftop usefulness,
scale, tenant neutrality, evidence and historical lineage, human
decision authority, entitlement/permission boundaries where applicable,
cross-domain ownership, and Relevant & Valuable Display.

Domain 2 Cleanup --- Resolved

Domain 2 owns canonical customer/vehicle volume, counts, populations,
and volume semantics.

Domain 6 owns management interpretation of customer demand, access,
appointment availability, scheduling, arrival, and customer-experience
consequences.

Appointments and Service Contacts may provide Evidence to both without
duplicate semantic ownership.

Domain 2 is formally cleared for freeze.

Consolidation Decisions

Metrics/KPIs are governed definitions over Evidence and
Outcomes, not foundational objects.

Opportunity remains first-class business meaning but is
implemented as a governed Condition type with lifecycle lineage.

Confidence concepts are governed dimensions, not foundational
objects.

Operational Wisdom is a mature Pattern state.

Economic Event is the canonical Event object with economic
classification and lineage.

Scenario is a reasoning construct over Assumptions, Conditions,
Outcomes, and proposed Decisions.

Root-Cause Compression is a reasoning doctrine.

Management Judgment is generally Declared Evidence.

No-Action is a legitimate Decision type/state.

Human management retains authoritative Decision rights.

Domain Vetting Summary

Domain 3 --- Service Sales & Production

Fundamental measurements map to Evidence and Outcomes. GP/RO, ELR,
Hours/RO, pace, and related measures belong in the Canonical Metric
Registry. VETTED / PASS.

Domain 4 --- Technician & Capacity

People, platform, process, Events, Conditions, production Outcomes,

Domain 5 --- Parts Operations

Quantity, velocity, quality, and availability fit the shared model.
Stock-out, backorder, obsolescence, excess inventory, and availability

Domain 6 --- Customer / Service Experience

Access → Communication → Promise & Convenience → Resolution & Quality →
Customer Outcome remains intact. CSI is an Outcome, not a diagnosis.
VETTED / PASS.

Domain 7 --- Sales / Opportunity Conversion

Opportunity becomes a governed Condition type with lifecycle Evidence,
Events, Relationships, Decisions, and Outcomes. ODI/OCI remain MI
reasoning capabilities. VETTED / PASS.

Domain 8 --- Workforce / People & Leadership

People are Entities; role assignments are effective-dated Relationships;
capability, dependency, continuity, readiness, and succession risks are
Conditions. Employment decisions remain human. VETTED / PASS.

Domain 9 --- Operating Control, Economic Integrity & Absorption

Economic Event consolidates into Event plus economic classification.
Accounting Recognition remains distinct from operating economic meaning.
Capability → Tenant Entitlement → Role Permission → Evidence
Availability → Relevant & Valuable Display remains intact. VETTED /
PASS.

Domain 10 --- Forecasting, Planning & Scenario

Actual, trajectory, forecast, plan, and scenario remain distinct. Future
outcomes become Outcome states; scenario/strategic assumptions use
Assumption; confidence remains dimensional. VETTED / PASS.

Domain 11 --- Organizational & Multi-Store

Scale-activated, not scale-required. One rooftop needs no artificial
hierarchy. At scale, Relationships, Patterns, aggregation integrity,
transferability, management altitude, and Operational Wisdom activate
naturally. VETTED / PASS.

Domain 12 --- Business & Strategic

Business-first and scale-neutral. Works for owner/operator, GM-led
store, small group, multi-store, regional, and enterprise structures
using the same canonical model. VETTED / PASS.

Scale Validation

One Rooftop: Full MI capability without enterprise hierarchy.

Small Group: Same objects plus cross-store Relationships,
comparison, shared resources, dependency, and allocation reasoning.

Multi-Store / Regional: Adds organizational Patterns, management
scope, transferability, aggregation integrity, and intervention
altitude.

Enterprise: Adds executive compression, entitlement, permission,
cross-organizational reasoning, strategic allocation, and governance.

One store does not run a simplified version of MI. Enterprise does
not run a different version of MI.

Scale changes scope, comparison context, organizational Relationships,
management altitude, permissions, entitlements, and display compression.
It does not change the underlying intelligence model.

Frozen Principles

Scale Neutrality Principle

Canonical MI intelligence remains useful without requiring a particular
organizational size, hierarchy, management layer, or operating
structure.

Business-First Intelligence Principle

MI begins with the business being managed and scales outward through
organizational context.

One Object Model, Multiple Intelligence Domains Principle

MI domains apply specialized reasoning to shared canonical objects and
do not maintain separate versions of business reality.

Decision Authority Principle

MI may identify, evaluate, recommend, and learn from alternatives, but
an authoritative management Decision requires an appropriately
authorized human or governed external decision process.

Historical Integrity Principle

Canonical objects that materially contributed to prior reasoning,
decisions, interventions, or outcomes retain historical state and
lineage when later corrected, superseded, invalidated, or reinterpreted.

Architecture Validation Milestone

All twelve Management Intelligence domains have now been

permission, and display compression, but does not change the
underlying intelligence model.

# ADR-004: Management Intelligence™ Shall Be Explainable

Status:
Accepted

Date:
July 2026

Authors:
Lonnie
ChatGPT

---

## Context

Managers will only trust recommendations they understand.

Black-box recommendations reduce confidence and adoption.

---

## Decision

Every recommendation produced by Management Intelligence™ shall include sufficient reasoning to explain why it was selected.

Recommendations should communicate:

- Why this issue matters
- Supporting operational evidence
- Expected operational outcome
- Expected financial impact
- Confidence level

---

## Rationale

Explainable recommendations build trust, improve coaching, simplify debugging, and reinforce learning.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Greater manager confidence
- Easier validation
- Improved coaching
- Better adoption

### Tradeoffs

- Additional implementation effort
- Larger recommendation objects

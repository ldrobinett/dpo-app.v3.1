## Context

Traditional dashboards emphasize historical reporting.

Fixed Operations leaders require operational decision support rather than additional reports.

Historical KPIs alone do not improve today's performance.

---

## Decision

The Home Dashboard will function as an operational briefing rather than a KPI reporting dashboard.

The dashboard presentation layer will remain stable while the Management Intelligence™ engine evolves beneath it.

---

## Design Principles

1. Action before information.

2. Today's decisions before historical reporting.

3. One operational workflow.

4. Every dashboard card answers a management question.

5. Recommendations lead users toward action.

## Design Philosophy

A dashboard tells you what happened.

Management Intelligence™ tells you what to do next.

ProdTracker intentionally favors operational guidance over historical reporting.

Every page should shorten the time between observation and corrective action.

Every recommendation should reduce the cognitive effort required of the manager.

The software exists to augment operational judgment, not replace it.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Faster decisions
- Reduced information hunting
- Operational consistency
- Foundation for Management Intelligence™

### Tradeoffs

- Greater dependence on the decision engine
- Increased business calculations
- Larger controller until service extraction

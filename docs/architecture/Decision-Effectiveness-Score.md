# Management Intelligence v5
## Decision Effectiveness Score (DES)

**Status:** Draft for architectural review
**Session:** 012
**Scope:** Canonical Decision Effectiveness Framework

---

# 1. Purpose

The Decision Effectiveness Score (DES) evaluates the effectiveness of Management Decisions.

Its purpose is not to judge leaders.

Its purpose is to improve leadership by separating:

- Decision Quality
- Execution Quality
- Business Outcomes
- Organizational Learning

These dimensions are evaluated independently because successful organizations improve all four.

---

# 2. Philosophy

Organizations often judge decisions solely by outcomes.

Management Intelligence rejects that approach.

A good decision can fail because of poor execution.

A poor decision can occasionally succeed through luck or exceptional effort.

True organizational learning requires understanding the difference.

---

# 3. Decision Effectiveness Model

Decision Effectiveness is composed of four independent dimensions.

Decision Quality

↓

Execution Quality

↓

Business Outcome

↓

Organizational Learning

Together these produce the Decision Effectiveness Score.

---

# 4. Decision Quality (30%)

Decision Quality evaluates the quality of leadership thinking.

Evaluation Criteria:

- Clear objective
- Quality evidence
- Root cause identified
- Alternatives considered
- Risks evaluated
- Assumptions documented
- Success measurements defined
- Appropriate urgency

Example Questions:

Did leadership solve the correct problem?

Did leadership understand the constraint?

Was sufficient evidence available?

---

# 5. Execution Quality (30%)

Execution evaluates management discipline.

Evaluation Criteria:

- Commitments completed
- Tasks completed
- Coaching delivered
- Standards followed
- Timeline achieved
- Resources allocated
- Accountability maintained

Execution measures behavior.

Not intentions.

---

# 6. Business Outcome (30%)

Business Outcomes evaluate business impact.

Possible outcomes include:

Financial

- Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Expense

Operational

- Productivity
- Efficiency
- Throughput
- Capacity

Customer

- CSI
- Retention
- Recommendation Acceptance

Leadership

- Team Capability
- Standard Adoption
- Process Stability

Outcomes should be measured against the expected result defined by the original Management Decision.

---

# 7. Organizational Learning (10%)

Every completed Management Decision contributes to organizational capability.

Learning evaluates:

- Lessons captured
- Best practices identified
- Knowledge shared
- Future recommendations improved
- Operating standards updated

A failed decision that generates valuable organizational learning is not a wasted decision.

---

# 8. Decision Effectiveness Matrix

| Decision | Execution | Outcome | Learning | Interpretation |
|-----------|-----------|----------|-----------|----------------|
| High | High | High | High | Best Practice |
| High | Low | Low | High | Execution Failure |
| High | High | Low | High | Incorrect Assumptions |
| Low | High | Moderate | Moderate | Strong Execution Masked Weak Decision |
| Low | Low | Low | Low | Fundamental Leadership Opportunity |

Every outcome should produce different coaching.

---

# 9. Decision Effectiveness Score

Example weighting.

Decision Quality             30%

Execution Quality            30%

Business Outcome             30%

Organizational Learning      10%

Organizations may adjust weighting based upon strategic objectives.

---

# 10. Decision Maturity

Management Decisions mature through experience.

Level 1

Reactive

Responds after problems occur.

---

Level 2

Operational

Uses measurements and evidence.

---

Level 3

Analytical

Uses intelligence to anticipate issues.

---

Level 4

Predictive

Uses organizational learning to prevent issues.

---

Level 5

Adaptive

Continuously improves leadership capability through validated learning.

---

# 11. Organizational Coaching

DES should improve coaching.

Examples:

Low Decision Quality

↓

Improve evidence.

Improve problem definition.

---

Low Execution Quality

↓

Improve accountability.

Improve follow-up.

---

Low Business Outcome

↓

Review environmental factors.

Review strategic alignment.

---

Capture lessons.

Update standards.

Share best practices.

# 14. Foundational Principle

Management Intelligence evaluates management effectiveness rather than management outcomes alone.

The platform measures:

How well leadership thinks.

How well leadership executes.

How well the business responds.

How well the organization learns.

Together these define sustainable management excellence.

---

# 15. Philosophy

Great organizations are built one management decision at a time.

Decision Effectiveness is not a measure of success.

It is a measure of the organization's ability to consistently transform evidence into disciplined action, disciplined action into measurable outcomes, and measurable outcomes into enduring organizational wisdom.

That capability is the true competitive advantage of Management Intelligence.

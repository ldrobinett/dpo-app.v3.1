# Management Intelligence v5
## Canonical Decision Pipeline

**Status:** Frozen for Phase 1B  
**Branch:** `mi-v5`  
**Decision Date:** 2026-07-25  
**Scope:** September 2026 MVP operating pipeline

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the single canonical pipeline by which Management Intelligence converts business facts into accountable decisions and measured outcomes.

No dashboard, AI process, workflow, meeting, service, or implementation may create a parallel pipeline with competing meanings.

---

# 2. Canonical Pipeline

```text
Business Source Data
        ↓
Measurement
        ↓
Derived Metric
        ↓
Evaluation against Objective and/or Operating Standard
        ↓
Constraint, Risk, or Opportunity classification
        ↓
Intelligence Finding
        ↓
Recommendation Output
        ↓
Management Decision
        ↓
Commitment and/or Action
        ↓
Execution Evidence
        ↓
Validation
        ↓
Outcome
```

Organizational Learning is post-MVP. The MVP preserves validated history so learning can be added later without redesigning the decision record.

---

# 3. Stage Contract

## 3.1 Business Source Data

Source systems provide operational, financial, customer, workforce, or service facts.

Source data is not automatically trusted as interpreted business meaning. It must be mapped, timestamped, and traceable.

## 3.2 Measurement

A Measurement is an atomic observed or imported business fact.

Required contract:

- Measured object
- Time or Financial Period
- Definition
- Unit
- Source
- Evidence lineage

## 3.3 Derived Metric

A Derived Metric is a reproducible calculation from governed Measurements or upstream Derived Metrics.

Required contract:

- Calculation method and version
- Input references
- Evaluation context
- Reproducible output

## 3.4 Evaluation

Evaluation compares a Measurement or Derived Metric with an Objective, Operating Standard, governed baseline, or approved comparison rule.

Evaluation produces a performance condition. It does not itself create a management recommendation or decision.

## 3.5 Constraint, Risk, or Opportunity

- Constraint: a present condition limiting performance or execution.
- Risk: a possible future condition threatening an Objective or Commitment.
- Opportunity: an identified potential for improved performance.

A condition must be classified explicitly. Ambiguous labels such as issue, alert, concern, or insight may appear in the interface but may not replace the canonical classification.

## 3.6 Intelligence Finding

An Intelligence Finding is the evidence-backed explanation of what matters, why it matters, and where it applies.

Required contract:

- Organizational context
- Evidence references
- Measurement or Derived Metric references
- Classified condition
- Explanation
- Confidence or limitation when inference is involved

An Intelligence Finding may exist without a Recommendation Output when evidence is insufficient, action is unnecessary, or the finding is informational.

## 3.7 Recommendation Output

A Recommendation Output is a proposed management response generated by the system or an approved rule.

Required contract:

- Supporting Intelligence Finding
- Proposed response
- Expected effect
- Priority or urgency
- Relevant assumptions
- Evidence trace

A Recommendation Output is advisory. It may never silently approve itself.

## 3.8 Management Decision

A Management Decision is an accountable human choice.

The manager may:

- Accept a Recommendation Output
- Modify it
- Reject it
- Defer it
- Originate a Decision independently when supported by Evidence

Required contract:

- Accountable owner
- Decision timestamp
- Organizational context
- Evidence or Intelligence Finding reference
- Decision disposition
- Rationale

## 3.9 Commitment

A Commitment is an explicit accountable promise resulting from a Management Decision.

Required contract:

- Owner
- Due date
- Success measure
- Status
- Decision trace

## 3.10 Action

An Action is specific execution work resulting from a Decision or Commitment.

Required contract:

- Owner
- Expected completion condition
- Status
- Due date or execution window when applicable
- Decision or Commitment trace

## 3.11 Execution Evidence

Execution Evidence proves what was done, when, and by whom.

Status alone is not sufficient evidence once an Action is claimed complete, except where the system records an explicit approved exception.

## 3.12 Validation

Validation is the governed comparison of expected and actual performance.

Required contract:

- Evaluation target
- Expected result
- Observed Measurement or Derived Metric
- Evaluation period
- Method
- Result

## 3.13 Outcome

An Outcome records the observed result of a Decision, Commitment, or Action.

Required contract:

- Trace to Decision, Commitment, or Action
- Observed result
- Supporting Evidence
- Time context
- Validation reference when applicable

An Outcome may show association. It may not claim causation without an approved causal method.

# 4. Permitted Branches

The canonical pipeline allows controlled branches:

- A Finding may produce no Recommendation Output.
- A Decision may reject or defer a Recommendation Output.
- A Decision may create a Commitment, an Action, both, or neither when rejection/deferment is recorded.
- A Commitment may exist before its Actions are fully planned.
- Validation may occur more than once.
- Multiple Outcomes may be recorded across time.

These branches do not create alternate pipelines. They remain traceable to the same canonical stages.

---

# 5. Prohibited Bypasses

The MVP must not:

1. Create a Recommendation Output without evidence-backed reasoning.
2. Convert a Recommendation Output directly into an approved Management Decision.
3. Mark a Commitment successful solely because Actions were marked complete.
4. Create an Outcome without traceability to the management work being evaluated.
5. Present inference as Measurement.
6. overwrite historical Evidence, Decisions, Validations, or Outcomes to improve the appearance of performance.
7. treat a dashboard alert as a Decision.
8. claim Organizational Learning before sufficient validated history exists.

# 6. Initial Automotive Service Vertical Slice

```text
Repair Order and Operation facts
        ↓
Service Measurements
        ↓
Derived performance metric
        ↓
Evaluation against Objective or Operating Standard
        ↓
Constraint, Risk, or Opportunity
        ↓
Intelligence Finding
        ↓
Recommendation Output
        ↓
Manager Decision
        ↓
Commitment and Action
        ↓
Validation against later service performance
        ↓
Outcome
```

The service source domain proves the pipeline. It does not define the universal architecture.

---

# 7. Freeze Rule

This pipeline is frozen for the September 2026 MVP. Existing architecture documents must conform to this document where terminology or stage order conflicts. Implementation may optimize processing, storage, and presentation, but may not bypass or redefine the canonical business stages without change control.

Management Intelligence™ Principles

#  Structure precedes intelligence

1. Measure potential before measuring performance.

2. Organizational health predicts financial health.

3. Constraints determine throughput.

4. Decisions should be financially explainable.

5. Recommendations should be measurable.

6. The system should learn from outcomes.

7. Experience should become organizational knowledge.

8.  Management Intelligence™ exists to transform operational data into managerial judgment.

9.  Management Intelligence™ never tells a manager what happened. It tells a manager what to do next, and why.

10.  # Architectural Oath

Management Intelligence™ will never exist to create more reports.

Every feature must improve a management decision.

If a feature does not improve a decision, it does not belong in the Management Intelligence™ engine.
